ScienceScience & CS

  • Nature (Westlake University and others): Pinal, a 16B-parameter model, designs proteins from plain-language descriptions of function. It worked on all four tasks tested, including two active enzymes, and a designed H-protein raised titre 1.7x in a CO2-fixation pathway with no lab optimisation. The weights are open under Apache 2.0. Nature
  • Nature: a 3D-printing method grows true single crystals of metals and alloys in complex shapes up to one metre long on readily available equipment, which matters for turbine blades and other parts. Nature News & Views
  • Nature (Stanford, 87 implanted patients over a decade): the most detailed map yet of the insula. The rear produces localised pain and heat, the middle visceral feelings like nausea, and the front links to cognition. "Not just reading the body," says Josef Parvizi. Nature news
  • Nature: the first switching of an all-antiferromagnetic tunnel junction by its own current ("octupole-driven spin-transfer torque") in PtMn3, with 363% magnetoresistance at room temperature. It points to denser magnetic memory and terahertz devices. Nature

Data & CloudData engineering & AI cloud

  • DuckDB 2.0.0 LTS "Cinnamon Teal" is out (GitHub, Oct 21), built from close to 20,000 commits. Highlights: the Quack protocol plus CONNECT for client-server, async I/O for Parquet, CSV and native files on S3, shredded VARIANT, triggers, a PEG parser by default, and a CLI agent mode that cut model-read output by 59% on TPC-H. Caveats: pre-2.0 builds can't read new database files, so wait for 2.0.1 (Nov 18) if the workload is mission-critical. LTS runs to Oct 2027 and 1.5 is extended to March 2027. DuckDB, release
  • ClickHouse shipped another four patch releases on Oct 21 (v26.9.24.8, v26.8.33.10-lts, v26.7.36.9, v26.3.58.5-lts), and the 26.10 "JOIN release" call is today at 17:00 CEST. Polars (2.0.0, Oct 6) and chDB (v4.4.0, Sep 11) are quiet. ClickHouse releases
  • Databricks made AI Runtime GA: serverless multi-node GPUs inside the lakehouse with second-level launches and Unity Catalog governance (Robinhood, FactSet, Flo Health already use it). Cloudflare added full-transaction WAF detection and response rules, free on all plans, starting with the php2shell flaw. AWS is integrating Nvidia's Ising quantum models into AWS PCS. Databricks, Cloudflare, Nvidia
