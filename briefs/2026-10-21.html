Google Developers Blog, SiliconANGLE

Reuters on the WSJ report, TNW on Prometheus

Red SeaRed Sea shipping

  • Britain will deploy RAF Typhoons to protect Saudi cities and infrastructure from Houthi drones and missiles (no offensive strikes in Yemen), plus an A400M and advisers, and it extended its air-to-air refuelling offer by 90 days. Riyadh's airport has been targeted 20 times since the start of October, with at least 18 killed. DW / Reuters
  • The toll from Monday's Houthi ballistic missile strike on a government training camp in Shabwa, home to the Balhaf LNG terminal, rose to 73, with 14 missing. Officials fear a ground push like the one that took Mocha. The Houthis say Saudi jets killed eight fishermen off Dhubab, near Bab el-Mandeb. AFP via RTHK
  • Carriers keep heading back anyway: Gemini (Maersk and Hapag-Lloyd) launches a Suez-routed China to West Med service (AE18/SE5) on Nov 22. Hapag-Lloyd's Habben Jansen expects "more or less all" Gemini services back through Suez in Q1 2027; Lars Jensen puts market-wide normalisation at only about 30-35% so far. The Loadstar
  • Risk picture: UANI, citing JMIC data, says Red Sea transits are slowly rising as direct ship strikes ease, even as the Saudi-Houthi war escalates on land. Drewry warns that a war-risk premium of just 0.3% of hull value would wipe out the roughly $1m round-voyage saving of Suez over the Cape. Next Drewry WCI is Thursday. UANI Oct 20, Drewry via Hellenic Shipping News

ScienceScience & CS

  • Anthropic, Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience: Claude autonomously designed protein minibinders against five scorpion neurotoxins. 17 of 211 lab-tested designs met the binding criteria, the best two (Cn2-B1, Cn2-B2) at single-digit nanomolar. Neutralisation is untested, and all designs and data are on Hugging Face. Anthropic, dataset
  • Nature (Tsinghua, Dai Qionghai): Airscope, a 1-gram wireless head-mounted microscope, records more than 8,500 neurons across 14 cortical areas in freely roaming mice, about 25x prior wireless devices, for over 200 days. China.org.cn (state media) on the Nature paper
  • Nature news: OneStrata, an AI model trained on nearly 4M fossils, suggests three of the "Big Five" mass extinctions were gradual declines, not sudden cataclysms. It was presented at the GSA meeting, and outside paleontologists are cautious. Nature
  • Physics: the first experimental demonstration of attosecond hard X-ray pulses (Nature Photonics), and the first direct observation of the long-elusive Lindhard electron-hole continuum, in MgB2 via ultra-soft RIXS (Nature Communications). Nature Photonics, Nature Comms
