AIAI build-out & tools

  • Nscale is in talks to sell Anthropic computing power in a deal worth about $45bn for a second facility at its Monarch campus in West Virginia (Bloomberg, not independently verified by Reuters). Reuters also confirmed Microsoft's exit from the Loughton, Essex site: 23,040 GB300 GPUs and 50 MW were due early next year, but grid power is now not expected until the 2030s; Nscale filed for a US IPO last month. Reuters on the $45bn talks, Reuters on Microsoft and Loughton
  • SemiAnalysis "Datacenters 2027: The Year of Acceleration": it forecasts +50GW in a single year, with about 60GW under construction, more than 24GW at 40 campuses already in advanced construction, and slightly under $1T of binding datacenter leases signed in 2026 so far. It directly rebuts Brad Gerstner's "about 25GW" hunch from All-In and flags memory and logic as the possible #1 constraint in 2027-28. SemiAnalysis newsletter
  • Tenstorrent (Jim Keller) closed a $1.5bn Series E at a $6.2bn post-money valuation, more than double its late-2024 mark, led by Trace Capital and Innovation X, to scale its Galaxy AI servers and RISC-V IP licensing. Microsoft also signed a 20-year, roughly 1.1GW power deal with NiSource for a northern Indiana campus starting in 2028. Tenstorrent, TechCrunch, NiSource deal (via DCD)
  • New models and policy: Nvidia released open-weight Nemotron 3.5 Super VL (120B, for enterprise agents) and the Bolt 74B-A9B model for local agents on RTX Spark AI PCs and DGX Spark (Bolt E4B later this month). Separately, DHS ordered federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek products and stop buying them; the order was posted Oct 19. TechCrunch on Nemotron 3.5, BBC on the DeepSeek ban

Red SeaRed Sea shipping

  • The Saudi-Houthi war is escalating on land and in the air. The Houthis claim six attacks in 48h on Aramco facilities in Yanbu (on the Red Sea coast), the Shaybah field, Riyadh and Abha airports and radar sites. A Houthi ballistic missile strike on a government camp in Shabwa killed at least 53 recruits and wounded about 200. Riyadh postponed its FII conference (due Oct 26-29) to 2027 and keeps government staff working from home. Xinhua roundup, AP via NBC, Al Jazeera
  • At sea it is still tense but without new hits: the German Navy's weekly report finds no further confirmed attacks on merchant ships around Bab el-Mandeb since its last update, six ships hit since the July 20 Houthi blockade of Saudi-linked vessels, and the Houthis still able to strike from inland. In Hormuz, Iran is now increasingly targeting container and cargo ships, not just tankers. German Navy report via World Ports Org
  • Egypt's parliament again delayed its vote on authorising military support for Saudi Arabia, now to Oct 26. An overseas combat mission needs a two-thirds majority, and the National Defence Council has already approved. Al Jazeera
  • Traffic is still coming back (latest prints from last week, next Drewry on Thursday): Clarksons via BIMCO puts Suez container capacity at 753k TEU a week in early October, 52% of 2023 levels, as Gemini moves Asia-Med loops back. Drewry's WCI rose 3% to $4,485/FEU and Shanghai-Rotterdam rose 6% to $3,522, ending a 13-week Asia-Europe decline. Maritime News weekly

ScienceScience & CS

  • Google DeepMind on the Busy Beaver frontier (arXiv 2610.22603): a Gemini agent pipeline (pattern detection, conjecture, informal proof, then Lean 4) found and formally verified Collatz-like accelerations for 11 previously unanalysed BB(6) holdouts, provisionally new "Cryptids", and proved 15 more the community had only conjectured. arXiv
  • Nature Aging (Sereda et al., with a News & Views): chaperone-mediated autophagy declines with age, raising the senescent-cell burden partly because macrophages stop clearing those cells, which promotes tissue dysfunction and systemic ageing. Nature Aging
  • Nature Genetics: a map of alternative polyadenylation (3' mRNA end choice) across 379 human brains links genetic variation in 3'-UTR choice to brain-disease risk loci, a regulatory layer that RNA abundance alone misses. Nature Genetics
  • Nature research highlights: an improved optical cavity photon collector could link several neutral-atom quantum arrays into larger machines (Stanford), and a US rapid chemical-fingerprinting programme is tracking a widening range of toxic adulterants in street fentanyl. quantum interconnect, fentanyl

Data & CloudData engineering & AI cloud

  • ClickHouse shipped three more patch releases on Oct 19: v26.9.22.3-stable, v26.8.31.2-lts and v26.3.56.2-lts. On the blog, Postgres wait events are now in Query Insights for ClickHouse Managed Postgres (with an "ask ClickHouse Agents" flow), and Jump Trading described running 80-200 TB a day of order and trade logs on ClickHouse for 7+ years.   • Cloudflare cut the cost of its internal AI code reviewer by 68%, from $2.43 to $0.77 per review, and the median review time fell from 4m09s to 1m32s. About 40% came from sharing one cached prompt prefix across repos and reviewers, plus fewer calls; the rest came from model and effort swaps (Sonnet 5.5 used 47-62% fewer tokens than Sonnet 5). It also previewed Clairic, an extraction model on Workers AI. Cloudflare blog, Clairic
  • Tailscale launched the MagicRoute beta: clients choose the nearest healthy subnet router or app connector by coarse location and stick with it, replacing flappy DERP-region routing (Premium and Enterprise). Vercel AI Gateway added Inception's Mercury 3 diffusion LLM and MiniMax M3.1 Flash (free); Databricks posted on what makes Unity Catalog open. Tailscale, Vercel Mercury 3, Vercel MiniMax, Databricks
  • No new releases from Polars (2.0.0, Oct 6), DuckDB (1.5.6, Sep 28) or chDB (v4.4.0, Sep 11), and the Polars, DuckDB and MotherDuck blogs are quiet since last week's Vera benchmarks and Azure waitlist. Polars, DuckDB, chDB
