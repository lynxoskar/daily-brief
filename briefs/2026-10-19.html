Anthropic, Sacks

Bloomberg via Straits Times, Reuters

  • arXiv 2610.21055 "Athanor": four Claude Opus 5.5 agents running on their own for 104 hours post-trained Qwen3-4B-Base from 15.9 to 67.3 on a dev suite and from 9.2 to 48.2 on a hidden held-out suite, edging the official Qwen3-4B-Instruct (66.5 / 47.9) by recombining known methods: SFT, RL, cross-agent weight merges and self-distillation. arXiv
